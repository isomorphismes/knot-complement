# m004 intrinsic CPU view

Origin: [Flexible Pipes issue 134](https://github.com/isomorphisms/flexible-pipes/issues/134).
Prerequisites are the checked H3 ray reference and M004-ATLAS-01. This slice
extends the repository's established close C reference. It introduces no
general scene engine, alternate compiler fallback, Android package, or Idriç
language implementation.

`c/m004_view.h` keeps a mathematical camera (point, orthonormal tangent frame,
tetrahedron representative, accumulated travel, field of view and aspect)
separate from raster dimensions. `camera_ray` normalizes a linear combination
of the three tangent-frame vectors. The resulting unit tangent generates a
geodesic in H3. Optical travel begins at zero; camera odometry remains intact.
Repeated image sampling cannot move the camera or change the atlas.

The scene supplies one optional labelled geodesic sphere at each tetrahedron's
centre. The radius must be positive and at least 1e-8 below the regular ideal
tetrahedron inradius, log(2)/2. Each admitted sphere is wholly inside its
geodesically convex tetrahedron. Therefore an intersection from a point in
that tetrahedron precedes every exit face, including a later ambiguous edge.
This containment rule is an intentionally narrow object interface. Objects
crossing a chart boundary would require additional object-image ownership and
clipping operations and are refused by this representation.

`trace` uses exact H3 sphere intersections and atlas face events. On an
identified face it transforms the point, tangent and complete frame, preserves
optical distance, and continues in the paired tetrahedron. An atlas face is
not drawn as an opaque wall. A ray that starts on a seam is reoriented by the
atlas: an outward direction changes representative at zero optical distance;
a tangent direction remains unresolved. Camera forward motion and yaw/pitch
use the same authoritative state operations.

The only illumination is an explicitly chosen camera headlight plus ambient
term. The cosine is the Lorentz inner product of the outward unit sphere
normal with the returning ray direction. This is useful shape shading. It is
not a light-transport, shadow, radiometric, GPU or physical-device claim.

Each trace has an intrinsic distance budget and a face-crossing budget. Hitting
either returns a separate status. Unresolved edge/tangent events and numerical
range refusals are separately colored. A crossing refusal reports the last
accepted state before the refused event; it does not label a chart face a
boundary of the manifold. Initial invalid inputs leave outputs unchanged.
The inherited numerical component limit is 64, and this driver advances at
most one intrinsic unit per segment, with a total budget no larger than 64.

## Supplied and computed information

The caller supplies the fixed m004 atlas, the camera and frame, normalized
sensor coordinates, sphere mask/radius, and two budgets. Software computes
geodesics, nearest admitted object hits, face transitions, transported frames,
and diagnostic colors. Field of view, sampling density, finite view distance,
object choices and headlight shading are explicit extra choices. None follows
from the metric alone.

An axial oracle starts in tetrahedron zero at (0,0,0,1), tangent
(-1,-1,-1,0)/sqrt(3), and looks at the sphere in tetrahedron one. The centre
separation through face zero is log(2). With radius log(2)/8, the first hit is
at 7 log(2)/8, after exactly one face identification. Its headlight cosine is
one. After moving the camera forward 1/2, the optical hit distance decreases
by 1/2 and the ray needs no face crossing. These quantities have meaning
independent of any pixels.

## Executable scope

`test_m004_view.c` checks that oracle, a local sphere before a face and before
a later tied edge, density-independent centre rays, camera state preservation,
movement and turning, explicit limits, unresolved color, and malformed inputs.
With `--emit PATH` it writes four ordered TSV fields for independent AI-CI:

| Field | Mathematical expectation |
|---|---|
| axial_marker_distance | 7 log(2)/8 |
| axial_marker_crossings | 1 |
| axial_marker_id | 1 |
| axial_headlight_cosine | 1 |

`render_m004.c OUTPUT.ppm RECEIPT.tsv` makes a nine-frame contact sheet, each
96 by 64, with successive camera positions spaced by 0.09 intrinsic units.
The scene contains the tetrahedron-one sphere of radius 0.22, the camera has
aspect 1.5 and tangent half vertical field of view 0.65, and view budgets are
distance four and 24 crossings. Every frame reports six status counts. The
image is a host reference artifact, not an interactive application. It makes
camera travel and quotient ray traversal executable before attaching controls
or a GPU implementation. Execution results belong to exact-head FP/AI-CI
receipts; source presence and compilation alone do not establish a pass.

## Existing native presentation and the remaining device work

Knot-complement main d0918cc2cf58fa0d01dac73cffab95686b2aab1e has no current
native Android scaffold. Mostow's living-sheet branch at
9c4bfd742f774c9b309df3acce049fc1aa930bf3 does have a NativeActivity/GLES2
presentation layer, Grease producers and paired A1/C67 target records:

- `android/mostow_android.c`, `android/mostow_renderer.c` and its renderer
  lifecycle interface show a reusable native presentation pattern.
- `android/build-pair.grease`, `ci/application-targets.tsv` and
  `ci/build-toolchain.tsv` distinguish armeabi-v7a MIRO A1 from arm64-v8a MIRO
  C67, using qualified ICK for owned C and NDK for platform glue/assembly/link.
- `native/mostow_view.h` is an extrinsic orbit camera. It must not become the
  intrinsic camera implementation by renaming it.
- `android/AndroidManifest.xml` identifies `org.isomorphisms.mostow`, library
  `mostow`. Reusing that package identity for this knot viewer would replace
  another application and is not part of this job.

The immediate native continuation is a separate knot viewer target with an
authoritative m004 camera, low-resolution CPU image upload through the retained
GLES2 lifecycle pattern, elapsed-time forward/turn controls, resizing that only
changes samples, and the existing qualified paired producer route. It needs an
actual package identity, signer and delivery record. A1 and C67 native builds,
GPU presentation, installation, launch, timing, face-crossing controls and
physical-device acceptance remain separate, unexecuted evidence kinds here.
No CPU image can count as those tests.
